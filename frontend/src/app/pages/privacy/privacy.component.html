Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Here's how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, such as your name, email address, and encrypted password. We also collect data about the links you shorten, including the original URLs, custom shortcodes, and click analytics (time of click, referring site, basic browser info).

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Fuipiz service, to process your link redirections, to provide you with link click analytics, and to communicate with you regarding your account.

3. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Passwords are cryptographically hashed, and link passwords are independently secured. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you logged in to your dashboard. We do not use third-party advertising trackers on the core service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@fupiz.com.
