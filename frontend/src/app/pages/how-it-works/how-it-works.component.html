How It Works

Simple, fast, and feature-rich link management.

1

Paste Your Link

Start by entering any long URL into the input field on the home page or in your dashoard. We accept URLs to websites, videos, images, and documents.

2

Customize Options

Don't just shorten it—own it. Create a custom alias (e.g., fupiz.com/my-link), secure it behind an encrypted password, or give the link an automatic expiration date for limited-time offers.

3

Share and Track

Copy your new short link or instantly download a generated QR code. Share it anywhere, and then return to your Dashboard to track lifetime click analytics.
