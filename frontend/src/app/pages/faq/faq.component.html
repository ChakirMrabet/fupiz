Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Fupiz.

Is Fupiz free to use?

Yes! Creating an account and shortening links is completely free. You get access to premium features like custom aliases, password protection, and click analytics at no cost. Advanced features and higher usage tiers are available with paid plans.

Can I edit or delete a short link after creating it?

Yes, you can edit or disable your links at any time from your dashboard. Change destinations, update settings, or remove links instantly. For security, some edits may be restricted on free plans.

How does password protection work?

When you set a password on a link, anyone who clicks the short URL will be redirected to an unlock screen. They must enter the correct password before being forwarded to your original destination. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text.

What happens when a link expires or reaches its click limit?

If you set an expiration date or click limit, the link will automatically stop working after the threshold is reached. Visitors will see a friendly message indicating the link is no longer active or has expired.

How does Fupiz handle privacy and data security?

We take privacy seriously. All passwords are hashed, analytics are anonymized, and we never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I use my own domain for short links?

Yes! With a paid plan, you can connect your own branded domain to create fully custom short links that match your brand and build trust with your audience.

How do payments and billing work?

All payments are securely processed by Stripe. You can manage your subscription, view invoices, and update payment methods from your dashboard at any time.

What support options are available?

We offer email support for all users and priority support for paid plans. Reach out to support@fupiz.com for help with your account, billing, or technical issues.

Can I use Fupiz for business or team projects?

Absolutely! Fupiz is built for both individuals and teams. Invite teammates, share analytics, and manage links collaboratively with advanced access controls.
