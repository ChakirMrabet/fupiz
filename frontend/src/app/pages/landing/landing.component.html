How It Works

Simple, fast, and feature-rich link management.

1

Paste Your Link

Start by entering any long URL into the input field. We accept URLs to websites, videos, images, and documents.

2

Customize Options

Don't just shorten it—own it. Create a custom alias, secure it behind a password, or give it an expiration date.

3

Share and Track

Copy your new short link or download a generated QR code. Share it anywhere, and track your click analytics.

Powerful Features

Everything you need to manage and grow your links, all from one dashboard.

🔗

Link Management

Create, organize, enable or disable your links instantly. Full control over every short URL you create.

📊

Click Analytics

Track how many times each link has been clicked. Monitor your reach and optimize your sharing strategy.

📱

QR Code Generation

Instantly generate downloadable QR codes for any link. Perfect for print materials, events, and signage.

🔒

Password Protection

Secure sensitive links behind a password. Only people with the correct password can access the destination.

⏰

Expiration Dates

Set auto-expiration on any link. Great for limited-time promotions, event registrations, or temporary access.

✏️

Custom Short Codes

Choose your own memorable alias instead of a random code. Make your links branded and recognizable.
